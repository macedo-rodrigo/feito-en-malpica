## Why nothing is sold here

Because it does not need to be. A small workshop does not need a payment
gateway, or a commission on every sale, or to explain to anyone how you
return a piece that was made to order. What it needs is for people to know it
exists and be able to ring it.

So this site does only half the job: it shows what there is and gives you the
phone number. The other half — the price, the timescale, the delivery, the
deal — gets sorted out by talking, the way it always was.

## Who can be listed

Anyone who works with their hands and has a workshop in Malpica de
Bergantiños or its parishes: Barizo, Cerqueda, Cambre, Leiloio, Mens, Buño,
Seaia and Vilanova. It makes no difference whether you have been at it thirty
years or two, or whether it is your main job or the thing you do after it.

There is no fee and no selection by style. Only two conditions: that you make
the pieces yourself, and that you answer the people who write to you.

## Who is behind it

It is a neighbourhood project, not-for-profit and with no advertising. The
site is static and collects nobody's data: no cookies, no analytics, no forms
that store anything. When you write to a maker, you write from your own email
or your own phone.

## The seal

The seal in the header is the profile of the Sisargas Islands seen from the
quay — the two islands you can pick out from the village on any clear day. It
goes black on light, white on dark, and always with the same handwriting.
