Malpica is a harbour village halfway along the Costa da Morte, with the
Sisargas Islands in front of it and the open Atlantic behind them. It lived
on fishing and, for the best part of a century, on whaling too: the Caneliñas
station is just down the coast in Cee, and there are people here who still
remember the boats coming into the quay.

There is still a fish market, still percebeiros working the Penedos rocks,
still people mending gear in the shade of the sheds. And alongside those old
trades there are new workshops: someone printing a part nobody manufactures
any more, someone firing ceramics in a small kiln, someone working wood the
way the gamelas were built.

This site is only that: somewhere to see them together, and to find out how
to reach them.
