## What we need from you

Not much, and we can help you put it together:

- **Your name and the workshop's**, if it has one.
- **What you make**, told the way you would tell someone on the quay.
- **Where you are**, and whether people can come and see the workshop.
- **How you want to be contacted**: phone, WhatsApp, email, or all three.
- **Photos of five or six pieces**, with prices or an "ask".
- Whether you **take commissions** and whether you **ship**.

The photos do not have to be studio work. The light in the workshop doorway
and a phone camera is plenty; if you have none at all, we will come and take
them.

## What we do

We build your page, write the text with you, and publish it. From then on it
is yours: if your number changes, if your prices go up, if you want different
photos, tell us and it changes the same day.

If one day you would rather not be listed, we take it down. That is all.
