**Nadia3D** is a 3D printing workshop in Malpica. Two kinds of thing come out
of it. Articulated figures, which move the moment you pick them up, joint by
joint, with no screws and no glue. And models painted by hand, one at a time,
with the detail put in with a fine brush.

Nearly all the work is made to order. If you like a piece but want it in a
different size, a different colour, or with something changed, say so and it
gets made. Pieces can also be modelled from a photograph or a drawing.
