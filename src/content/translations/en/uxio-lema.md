Uxío spent ten years repairing marine engines on the waterfront before he
bought his first printer. It started out of necessity: a workmate needed a
winch cover nobody manufactured any more, and there was nowhere to buy one.

Out of that came **Capa a Capa**, a small workshop on Rúa do Cantón that runs
in two directions. Mornings tend to be harbour work: parts that do not exist
on the market, broken pieces that have to be rebuilt from a fragment and a
photograph. Afternoons are the other side of it, the things made for the
pleasure of making them: lamps, models, miniatures of the village's boats.

Everything here is designed in Malpica and printed on the premises, with
nobody in between. Uxío works mostly in PLA and recycled filament, and for
the past year in liquid clay as well, fired in a small kiln in the workshop
itself.

If you have a broken part, bring it in. If you have an idea, bring that too:
it almost always goes better talked through at the bench than over messages.
